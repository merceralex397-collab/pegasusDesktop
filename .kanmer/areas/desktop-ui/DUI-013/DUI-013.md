## What

Move the screen specification blocks from `docs/desktop/06-ui-design/screen-specs.md` into FRD-13 (desktop operator experience) as its numbered sections with stable anchors, cross-reference FRD-12, and link every plan 05 slice ticket to the anchor of the screen it builds.

## Why

The plan set is programme planning, not authority — required behaviour becomes an FRD (`docs/desktop/README.md` preamble; `AGENTS.md` § New Markdown placement). Until the screens live in an FRD, a slice ticket has no governing document and cannot leave `backlog` under the `feature` profile's `governing-doc` gate. Operator-visible consequence: the screens a slice builds are the ones an accepted document describes, not a plan that can drift. Serves [[DSK-05-01]] through [[DSK-05-21]].
