## What

Move the screen specification blocks from `docs/desktop/06-ui-design/screen-specs.md` into FRD-13 (desktop operator experience) as its numbered sections with stable anchors, cross-reference FRD-12, and link every plan 05 slice ticket to the anchor of the screen it builds.

## Why

The plan set is programme planning, not authority — required behaviour becomes an FRD (`docs/desktop/README.md` preamble; `AGENTS.md` § New Markdown placement). Until the screens live in an FRD, a slice ticket has no governing document and cannot leave `backlog` under the `feature` profile's `governing-doc` gate. Operator-visible consequence: the screens a slice builds are the ones an accepted document describes, not a plan that can drift. Serves [[DSK-05-01]] through [[DSK-05-21]].

## Implementation steps

1. Orientation. Read the plan row, all of `docs/desktop/06-ui-design/screen-specs.md`, `docs/frd/README.md` and `docs/frd/frd-12-operator-experience.md`. Confirm `DSK-00-08` has landed and `docs/frd/frd-13-desktop-operator-experience.md` exists; if it does not, stop and record the block. Call Kanmer `get_doc_gates` for this ticket id, then `take_ticket`.
2. Load `pegasus-desktop`, then `kanmer-docs`. Follow the existing FRD conventions in `docs/frd/frd-12-operator-experience.md` for frontmatter, heading depth and cross-reference style — the existing convention wins (`AGENTS.md` § Simplicity rails).
3. Adopt the blocks into FRD-13, one section per screen, keeping the capability grouping of the source (§13.1 Access and session, §13.2 Dashboard and work queues, §13.3 Case lifecycle, §13.4 Intake, §13.5 Vehicle, §13.6 Parties and reference data, §13.7 Documents, §13.8 Communications, §13.9 Assessment and reporting, §13.10 Administration and operations) plus the Shell block. Adopt the § AutomationId convention and the § Cross-cutting state contract as their own sections — slice tickets need to cite both.
4. Give every section a stable anchor derived from its heading, and record the anchor list in `docs/desktop/06-ui-design/screen-specs.md` so a plan reader can jump to the authority. Anchors are stable across releases — do not renumber sections later.
5. FRD-13 cites `docs/design/README.md` as the binding design authority and does not restate its tokens, copy rules or accessibility list. Where the desktop deviates from proposal § 14 (Mica, global search, notification centre, minimum window width), FRD-13 carries the recorded deviation exactly as `docs/desktop/06-ui-design/README.md` § 3 states it.
6. Add the cross-reference in `docs/frd/frd-12-operator-experience.md` pointing to FRD-13 for desktop behaviour, and add the FRD-13 row to `docs/frd/README.md` and `docs/index.md` if `DSK-00-08` has not already.
7. Decide the source-of-truth rule and write it once, at the top of FRD-13: the FRD is authority, `docs/desktop/06-ui-design/screen-specs.md` becomes a pointer rather than a second copy. Replace each adopted block in the plan file with a one-line link to its FRD-13 anchor — do not leave two full copies (`AGENTS.md` § Simplicity rails, one list per concept).
8. Wire the board: for each plan 05 slice ticket (`DSK-05-01` … `DSK-05-21`) use Kanmer `search_items` to find its board id, then `link_doc` it to `docs/frd/frd-13-desktop-operator-experience.md` and record the specific anchor in the ticket body's Source of truth block. A slice ticket with no screen (for example a pure concurrency-UX ticket) links the state-contract anchor instead.
9. Verify the gate actually opens: run Kanmer `get_doc_gates` on one linked slice ticket and confirm the `governing-doc` requirement for `leave-backlog` is satisfied by the ref rather than by `docs_todo`.
10. Run `pwsh ./scripts/Test-DocumentationLinks.ps1` and `pwsh ./scripts/Test-MarkdownPlacement.ps1`. Both must pass; a broken anchor is a failure, not a warning.
11. Record in the ticket which screen blocks had no FRD home (if any) and why, rather than inventing a section for them.
