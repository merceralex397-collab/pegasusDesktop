## What

Stop producing `manifest.sha256` and `provenance.json` at all, and indent the exported EVA JSON. Not merely omitted from the archive: `WriteProvenance`, `WriteManifest` and the two filename constants leave `src/Pegasus.Core/Eva/EvaBundleSchema.cs`, the three members leave the `EvaBundle` record, and the three columns leave `EvaHandoffRevisions`. The package becomes the ordered thirteen-key JSON plus `Images/`, two-space indented with `\r\n` newlines, on every packaging path.

## Why

Exporting `ap.QDOS26015` on 2026-08-24 produced a package EVA will not import: ours is minified and carries two companion files, while every known-good sample is two-space indented and carries none. **The desktop inherits that failure unchanged** — `docs/desktop/05-implementation-and-migration/reuse-map.md:35` and `:65` mark `Eva/` (`EvaBundleSchema` 916 lines, `CaseEvaMapping`) REUSE server-side into slice S15, so the native client ships byte-identical output through the same Core writer.

**No seeded conversion ticket is permitted to make the fix.** [[DSK-05-15]] (FEAT-015) is the only board ticket that touches EVA at all, and its scope boundary reads: "Must not change `src/Pegasus.Core/Eva/EvaBundleSchema.cs`, `CaseEvaMapping` or `EvaHandoffStore.cs` — this ticket asserts the bundle's content, upstream `ENG-014` and `ENG-015` fix it." Its acceptance criterion ("The generated EVA bundle's entry list and JSON layout diff clean against `reference/eva_information/AX_SP58WVO.json`") is a **detector**, not a fix: without this ticket the slice can only report the failure and stop.

Nothing brings the fix in by itself either. Upstream ENG-014 lives on branch `task/eng-014-drop-manifest-indent-json` as PR #527 against upstream **`dev`**, not `main`, so it is outside the 32-commit `main` range [[DSK-01-10]] pins. Under **D-001** the fork becomes the single release source at the first production gateway change and upstream is frozen after one more merge — anything unmerged upstream is simply lost. This fix exists on the fork only if this ticket holds it.

Operator-visible consequence: an engineer cannot hand a case to EVA. Pegasus reports a successful generation and download, and the rejection happens outside Pegasus, where nothing records it.
