## What

Record decision **D-001** (2026-08-23, Option A) — at the first production gateway change the fork becomes the single release source for gateway, worker and desktop, and upstream `collisionengineers/pegasus` is merged one final time and then frozen — in ADR-0100's consequences and in `docs/operations.md`, and agree the freeze itself, with a date, with the upstream repository's owners.

## Why

D-001 is decided but currently lives only in planning documents (`docs/desktop/README.md` § Locked decisions and `docs/desktop/00-governance-and-workflow/README.md` § 3). Planning documents are explicitly not authority (`AGENTS.md` § New Markdown placement), so an agent releasing the gateway from the fork after the first `/api/v1` change has nothing authoritative telling it that upstream is no longer the release source — and the upstream owners have not been asked to freeze anything. Proposal §6.3 requires that no permanent second Pegasus repository exists; the freeze is how that is honoured. Until the freeze is agreed and dated, the one-way sync of [[DSK-00-02]] must keep running, so this ticket also fixes when that stops.
