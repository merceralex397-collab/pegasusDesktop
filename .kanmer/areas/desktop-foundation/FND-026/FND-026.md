## What

Author, review and accept two decision records in the reserved conversion block: `docs/adr/0100-native-winui3-desktop-client.md` (a native WinUI 3 client is built inside this fork and is authorised to add new top-level projects) and `docs/adr/0104-online-required-no-offline-replication.md` (the desktop is online-required with a bounded local cache only), then add both rows to the `docs/adr/README.md` index table.

## Why

`AGENTS.md` § Product invariants forbids adding a new top-level project, runtime, deployment unit or migration stream without an accepted ADR proving the existing boundary cannot carry it. Every remaining ticket in this epic adds such projects, so no Phase 1 code may merge until ADR-0100 is accepted. ADR-0104 bounds what a workstation may keep locally, which [[DSK-02-06]] (DPAPI credential store, bounded cache) and [[DSK-02-11]] (diagnostics bundle) implement, and it is the record that stops a later ticket from adding a local replica database. Operator-visible consequence: without these records the reviewer has no authority to check the new solution shape against, and the conversion's own scope is unwritten. Proposal § 5.4 (solution structure) and § 11 (local state and offline behaviour) are the design target.
