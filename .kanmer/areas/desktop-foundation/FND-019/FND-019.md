## What

Close flow records 1 (staff authentication and session), 2 (database and migration bundle) and 3 (Microsoft Graph intake) in `docs/desktop/01-inventory-and-parity/flow-records.md`: answer every open question `Q1.1`–`Q1.4`, `Q2.1`–`Q2.3`, `Q3.1`–`Q3.3` with a code citation, or move it to `docs/open-decisions.md` as a named unresolved decision.

## Why

Proposal §24 Phase 0's third exit-gate item is verbatim: "No unresolved uncertainty exists around authentication, database or Graph intake." Those three flows are also the inputs to ADR-0102 (identity and desktop session), ADR-0106 (Graph intake stays central) and ADR-0109, which [[DSK-00-06]] cannot author until this ticket lands. Operator-visible consequence: guessing at token claims or refresh-token rotation produces a desktop that signs an operator out mid-case or keeps a disabled account alive — today `OnValidatePrincipal` re-checks `IsEnabled` on every request and that guarantee must survive the move to tokens. Area 04 builds the token flow directly from record 1; [[DSK-01-07]] closes records 4–6.
