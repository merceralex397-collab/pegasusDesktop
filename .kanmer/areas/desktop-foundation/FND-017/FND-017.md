## What

Bring the parity-matrix rows for proposal §13.4 (intake, including Triage, Unidentified, image intake and manual upload), §13.7 (documents and evidence) and §13.8 (communications) to `inventoried` or `legacy path retained`: `PAR-13`, `PAR-16`, `PAR-17`, `PAR-19`–`PAR-31`.

## Why

Intake and communications are the highest-volume operator surfaces and the ones with the most hidden command sets — `src/Pegasus.Web/Pages/Triage/Details.cshtml.cs` dispatches 13 commands behind one `OnPostActionAsync`, and `src/Pegasus.Web/Pages/Mail/Message.cshtml.cs` is 1,025 lines. Proposal §13.4/§13.7/§13.8 and §23.1 require each of those commands to be inventoried before a desktop screen is specified. Operator-visible consequence: a triage command that exists in the web app but is missing from the desktop leaves received items stranded, exactly the class of defect upstream `INTK-033` already reports in production. Skeleton from [[DSK-01-01]]; the Graph and Box flows behind these rows are completed by [[DSK-01-06]] and [[DSK-01-07]].
