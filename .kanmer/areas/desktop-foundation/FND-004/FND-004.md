## Why

Locked decision L-05 makes this board the executable form of the plan set: if a plan row has no ticket, the work silently disappears, and if a ticket exists twice the board double-counts an area's remaining work. The seeding session on 2026-08-24 created 208 tickets — the exact number of `| DSK-` rows across the thirteen area READMEs (00:13, 01:12, 02:16, 03:18, 04:15, 05:26, 06:16, 07:19, 08:19, 09:16, 10:18, 11:9, 12:11) — with `DSK-09-07` and `DSK-09-09` withdrawn and therefore absent. What remains is verification, the carry-over batch that could not be created before the upstream triage existed, and the join that lets a human read `DSK-05-14` in a plan and find `FEAT-0xx` on the board (proposal §25; the plan handle lives in the ticket title for exactly this reason). **Do not re-seed**: `create_item` is ungated and a second pass duplicates every handle. [[DSK-00-03]] verifies the board's groups and areas; this ticket verifies its tickets.
