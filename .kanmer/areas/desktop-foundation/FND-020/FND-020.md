## What

Close flow records 4 (Box custody), 5 (DVLA/DVSA vehicle lookup) and 6 (report rendering) in `docs/desktop/01-inventory-and-parity/flow-records.md`: answer `Q4.1`–`Q4.3`, `Q5.1`–`Q5.3`, `Q6.1`–`Q6.4` with a code citation or a dated official-documentation citation, or move each to `docs/open-decisions.md`.

## Why

These three flows decide what the desktop is allowed to do directly and what must stay behind the gateway. ADR-0107 (Box and DVLA/DVSA credentials stay behind the gateway; no long-lived provider secret in the package) and ADR-0108 (report rendering through an isolated non-UI WebView2 HTML→PDF path, gateway renderer retained until golden-file parity, L-03) cannot be authored by [[DSK-00-06]] and [[DSK-00-07]] until the record answers exist. Operator-visible consequence: guessing that the Box SDK can issue constrained short-lived transfer URLs, when it cannot, produces a desktop upload path that either fails or ships a provider secret; guessing WebView2 print fidelity produces reports that differ from the ones already sent to clients. [[DSK-01-06]] closes records 1–3; area 07 builds the integrations from these answers.
