## What

Write the six foundation ADRs of the conversion in the reserved block — ADR-0100 (native WinUI 3 client converted inside this fork), ADR-0101 (local-execution / cloud-authority split and the six-question cloud-justification test), ADR-0103 (gateway rather than direct database access), ADR-0104 (online-required, no offline replication), ADR-0105 (signed MSIX/App Installer plus the gateway minimum-version gate) and ADR-0110 (agent-skill pinning and the invocation/review protocol) — and add their rows to `docs/adr/README.md`.

## Why

Every other plan in the set cites these decisions as settled, but none of them exists as a repository ADR yet: `docs/adr/` holds ADR-0001…ADR-0029 only. Until they are written, each conversion ticket rests on a planning document, and `docs/desktop/` is explicitly **programme planning, not authority** (`AGENTS.md` § New Markdown placement). The proposal requires the decision set in §26 and an ADR for each deviation in §27 item 18. The reserved block ADR-0100…ADR-0110 exists because upstream `collisionengineers/pegasus` keeps issuing numbers below 0100 and a one-way sync would otherwise collide — the operator confirmed the block on 2026-08-23 and `AGENTS.md` § ADR conventions records it. [[DSK-00-06]] writes the four flow-derived ADRs, [[DSK-00-07]] writes ADR-0108, and [[DSK-00-08]] writes FRD-13 on top of these.
