## What

Bring the parity-matrix rows for proposal §13.3 (case lifecycle) and §13.6 (parties and reference data) to `inventoried`: `PAR-07`–`PAR-12` covering the twelve `src/Pegasus.Web/Pages/Cases/**` page models and every one of their handlers, plus `PAR-40` (Organizations) and `PAR-41` (Principals).

## Why

The case workspace is the first vertical slice and the concurrency model of the whole conversion (proposal §13.3, §10.4, §24 Phases 3–4). The case mutation envelope — `CaseId`, `ExpectedVersion`, `Actor`, `OperationKey`, `Reason`, `EditLeaseToken` — is the seam every desktop command must reproduce, and the lifecycle commands are explicit endpoints, never a generic action dispatcher (proposal §10.2). Operator-visible consequence: an un-inventoried lifecycle command becomes a case that cannot be held, reopened or archived from the desktop, or worse, an edit silently overwriting another operator's. Skeleton from [[DSK-01-01]]; the untested Core policies these rows expose feed [[DSK-01-12]].
