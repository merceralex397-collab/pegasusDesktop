## What

Add one recorded sentence to ADR-0100 stating that the three prior documents the proposal's authority order cites — *Pegasus Desktop Conversion Plan*, *Desktop Azure Conversion Plan* and *Recommended desktop API architecture* — do not exist in this repository, are not retrievable, and are therefore not an input to any conversion ticket.

## Why

Proposal §2 item 5 places those three documents **fourth in the authority order**, above generic skill guidance. They are not in the repository (`git ls-files` finds none of them, and `docs/` has no such file), so an agent that takes the authority order literally will either stall looking for them or, worse, invent their contents to justify a decision. The area plan already states the position — "The proposal's three 'prior documents' (§2 item 5) are **not** in the repository; they are not an input to any ticket" — but a plan is not authority (`AGENTS.md` § New Markdown placement). Recording it in ADR-0100 puts it where the authority chain can see it, and closes the last unresolved reference in the proposal's authority order. It is deliberately a single sentence: proposal §3 already reconciles the *ideas* those documents carried, so nothing of substance is lost.
