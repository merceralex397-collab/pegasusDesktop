## What

Bring the parity-matrix rows for proposal §13.5 (vehicle and inspection), §13.9 (assessment, valuation and reporting) and §13.10 (administration and operations) to `inventoried`: `PAR-14`, `PAR-15`, `PAR-18`, `PAR-27`, `PAR-32`–`PAR-39`, `PAR-43`, `PAR-45`, `PAR-46`.

## Why

These are the last un-inventoried capability groups, and two of them decide big conversion questions: the assessment/report row is the evidence base for ADR-0108 (report rendering moves to an isolated WebView2 path, L-03), and the administration rows carry eight upstream redesign tickets that become desktop screen specifications rather than new Razor work. Proposal §13.5/§13.9/§13.10 and §23.1 require a row per capability with real evidence. Operator-visible consequence: without `PAR-45`/`PAR-46` the desktop has no inventoried source for the status bar, the About/version surface or the `/api/v1` shapes that the 35 `pegasus_*` MCP tools already prove out. Skeleton from [[DSK-01-01]]; the report-rendering flow behind `PAR-15` is completed by [[DSK-01-07]].
