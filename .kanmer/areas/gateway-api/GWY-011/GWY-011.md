## What

Add the two-step upload-session mechanism (`POST …/upload-session` → `PUT` bytes → `POST …/complete`) and the endpoints that use it: staff uploads with their status and group registration, case document upload, removal, content download and export, custody retry, third-party vehicle-evidence confirmation, request-upload links, and EVA handoff generation and download.

## Why

Proposal § 10.2 names the upload session as the API's file mechanism and § 13.7 makes documents and evidence a primary workflow. Operator-visible consequence: a large or interrupted transfer can be resumed or abandoned cleanly instead of leaving a half-written receipt, and the desktop shows real progress. Today the equivalent Razor handlers take an `IFormFile` in one request with a 10 MiB per-file cap — a shape that gives no progress and no resume. One route in this set is different in kind: request-upload link create/revoke is composed today as a store that throws, so it is plumbing over an inactive capability until upstream CASE-022 (board [[CASE-002]]) activates it, and this ticket must say so rather than promise an operator a link it cannot issue. A second route is different in kind for the opposite reason: `GET /uploads/{receiptId}/status` is the payload the desktop's upload status screen reads, and as projected today it hides a scheduled retry behind **Received** and reports a case only when a `CaseIntakeLinks` row exists — the two dishonesty defects upstream INTK-001 was raised about. This ticket owns that payload, so it corrects both here (step 6) rather than leaving [[DSK-05-13]] to infer them client-side.
