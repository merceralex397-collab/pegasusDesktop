## What

Add the two-step upload-session mechanism (`POST …/upload-session` → `PUT` bytes → `POST …/complete`) and the endpoints that use it: staff uploads with their status and group registration, case document upload, removal, content download and export, custody retry, third-party vehicle-evidence confirmation, request-upload links, and EVA handoff generation and download.

## Why

Proposal § 10.2 names the upload session as the API's file mechanism and § 13.7 makes documents and evidence a primary workflow. Operator-visible consequence: a large or interrupted transfer can be resumed or abandoned cleanly instead of leaving a half-written receipt, and the desktop shows real progress. Today the equivalent Razor handlers take an `IFormFile` in one request with a 10 MiB per-file cap — a shape that gives no progress and no resume.
