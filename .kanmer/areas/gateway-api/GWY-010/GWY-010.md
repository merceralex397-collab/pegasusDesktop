## What

Add the received-items surface to `/api/v1`: the paged list and the receipt detail, the six named intake commands, the three case-association commands, and the three byte endpoints for source, assets and images.

## Why

Proposal § 13.4 makes intake triage a primary operator workflow and § 12.1 keeps the Graph intake worker central and unattended — the desktop reads and acts on what the worker has already received. Operator-visible consequence: an operator can work the received queue natively, open the original source bytes, and link a receipt to a case, while intake keeps arriving with every desktop closed. Byte endpoints must send `Content-Length`, `ETag`, range support, `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff` and a safe filename, because these are the paths that hand untrusted attachment bytes to a client.
