## What

Project the triage, unidentified, image-intake and operations surfaces onto `/api/v1`, turning the twelve-case action dispatcher in `Pages/Triage/Details.cshtml.cs` into twelve explicit named command routes, and adding the operations snapshot with its two audited retry/revoke commands.

## Why

Proposal § 10.2 states plainly: "Do not expose a generic 'execute action' endpoint… Workflow-changing operations should be explicit and auditable." The Razor page uses a string dispatcher today, which is fine for a form post and wrong for a contract — a generated client cannot type a string switch, and an audit reader cannot tell one action from another in the route. Operator-visible consequence: triage, unidentified resolution and the operations retry queue all work natively, and every action appears in history under its own name.
